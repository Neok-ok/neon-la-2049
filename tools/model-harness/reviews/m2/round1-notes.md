# Round 1 builder notes

First wedge: lofted hull, raked canopy, four enclosed drums, a skirt held off the ground, nose strip, tail strip, amber, and a low bar turned across the roof. No panels, no decal, no maps. 2848 tris. Box 5.45 × 2.32 × 1.36 m.

The blank close-ups and the gloss are the next cut, not a surprise. Night practicals were still sitting on the drum, which is why the flank went magenta.
