# Round 10 builder notes

Not the official score. Round 9 kept the filled roof and the covered drums. Surface, material, and film were still under the bar: the close-ups were a wavy shell, the canopy read as a cyan bubble, and the louvers read as a few bright slabs.

This cut keeps that silhouette. The glass is smoked metal instead of a teal fill. The rear deck is a field of thin dark fins in a black cavity, not proud satin bars. The nose and the rear quarter carry inset panels, and each drum face has a shallow ring instead of a blank disc or a deep bore. Paint roughness is flat, the normal is flat, and the albedo is broad grime and a few chips. Quantized UVs had been tiling that paint about fifteen times; the export keeps float UVs so the close-up is the paint, not a repeat.
