Fails:

- Needle nose instead of the blunt faceted snout
- Drum faces are concentric cyan/magenta rings, like a lit wheel or turbine
- Close-ups have no real panel gaps, louvers, gullwing cut, or wear
- One glossy black highlight, no matte/satin split or gap grime
- Amber lamp and low roof bar do not read; practicals skew neon

Notes:

- Silhouette: size is in range. Side view has a low wedge, larger nose drum, smaller tail drum, flat roof, and no fin. The tail is only partly squared, and the planform is one smooth wedge rather than the prop's hard corners.
- Surface: nose and pod close-ups are almost bare. The pod is stacked circular grooves, not a flat covered face. Gullwing seam, deck louvers, and panel breaks are missing; the shell shows. No readable POLICE livery.
- Lighting: white nose bar and red tail strip are present, and the rim row describes the drums and roofline. Amber and the low red/blue bar do not read. Cool light becomes cyan and magenta rings on the drum and a neon cast on the nose.
- Material: studio reads as uniform black clearcoat with one broad highlight. No matte-against-satin panels, no grime in seams, no scuffs. Night exposure crushes the body to a flat silhouette.
- Film: in the street and fire plates it stays a separate black object. It does not take rain, firelight, or dust, and the smooth skin plus colored rings would not hold up next to the stills.
- Reads as K: unequal covered drums and the low wedge identify the target without a caption. Beside the stills it is a simplified cousin: too sharp at the nose, too smooth on the skin, and too lit at the drums.
