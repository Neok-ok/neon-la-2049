Fails:

- Top plan is an open staple instead of the solid flat roof with a nose slot
- Gullwing cut and rear louver deck do not read
- Close-ups are a clean one-tone shell with almost no panel gaps or wear

Notes:

- Silhouette: Length, width, and height sit in range, and the side is a low wedge with a larger covered nose drum, a smaller tail drum, a raked canopy, and a squared tail. The front shows split cheeks. From above the center opens into a wide void, so the outline is a staple rather than the filled roof in the plan.
- Surface: The nose channel is actually open and the drum faces stay flat and covered, with no bright ring or slots. The flank lacks the deep gullwing cut, the rear deck lacks the louver field, and the pod close-up is a blank cylinder. Panel lines are few and shallow.
- Lighting: White nose strip, red tail strip, small ambers, and a low red/blue roof bar are in the right places, and the rim pass shows the wedge. Cool underglow is barely there, and the night frames crush the body down to the strips.
- Material: The value is charcoal, but the shader is even satin with one broad highlight. Gaps are clean and the studio views show no scuffs or matte-against-satin breakup.
- Film: The tiny flight frame could pass. Next to the side and rear stills the smooth shell, missing door cut, and missing louvers would not hold up in a rainy street shot.
- Reads as K: The wedge, split nose, twin covered drums, and police strips identify it without a caption. It reads as a simplified spinner, not as the prop in the stills.
