# Round 3 critic

Fresh pass. Saw the contact sheet, the three stills, and the rubric. No build notes and no earlier scores.

Fails:

- Drum faces are blank covered discs, missing the pressed cover detail the stills have, and they read as barrels rather than fairing into the skirt
- Rear deck has no readable louver field and the side has no readable gullwing cut
- Close-ups are coarse and clean, one dark shader with no gap grime
- Amber markers and cool underglow are missing at sheet size; several night views are unreadable blobs

Notes:

- Silhouette: wedge, flat roof, larger nose drum and smaller tail drum, and a squared red tail are all there, with no fin. Beside the stills the nose is less of a blade, the drums stick out as barrels instead of fairing into the skirt, and the side outline is a simple wedge rather than the cut-gem profile.
- Surface: nose and pod close-ups show a few hard creases and then stop. The pod reads as a smooth black circle, the tail lacks a readable louvered deck, and there is no readable gullwing seam or wear in the gaps.
- Lighting: white nose strip and red tail strip read, and the rim row traces the wedge and drums. Amber is easy to miss, the roof lamp reads as a speck from some angles, underglow is not obvious, and the night row is often too dark to show form or rain.
- Material: charcoal is the right family, but it is one broad highlight with no matte-against-satin split, no dust in the seams, and no scuffs on the drum face.
- Film: shape is related, but the blank drums, smooth tail, and clean shader would not hold up in a rainy street frame next to the stills.
- Reads as K: POLICE, the two covered drums, the wedge, and the nose and tail strips make it K's spinner without a caption. Recognisable, and still a maquette.

IoU, not shown to the critic: side 0.880, front 0.813, top 0.891. Top is the reconstruction. Side stays above 0.85 after the nose was blunted. Front is above 0.80. The front trace is a perspective estimate and is narrower than the 2.32 m model.
