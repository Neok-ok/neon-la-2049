Notes:

- Silhouette: Long low wedge, split nose, filled roof, squared louvered tail, and drums all read from the side, front, rear, and top. The prongs are a bit long and parallel, and the canopy arch is smoother than the filmed wedge.
- Surface: The diagonal flank cut, rear louver banks, panel breaks, and open nose slot read at orbit and from above. Drum faces are flat cylinders with a dark tire lip and no slots or glow. The cut is a single seam, the vents are flat, and the close-up tires are faceted with only light speckle.
- Lighting: White nose strips, a red tail strip, small amber tips, a low red-blue bar, and cool underglow are all in place. The rim row picks out the wedge, prongs, canopy, and drums. A few colored lamps sit deep in the channel.
- Material: Night shows gunmetal panel tone, a clear-coat roof streak, tinted reflective glass, and fine grime. Studio flattens that toward a more even dark shell, short of the dusty broken metal in the stills.
- Film: A rainy street frame at orbit would pass. Beside the stills the door is thinner, the drums plainer, and the body too clean for a hero match.
- Reads as K: Immediate from the split nose, housed drums, POLICE flank, louvers, and low light bar, without being told.
- Stray parts: No.
- Wheels in prongs: Each front drum sits inside its prong, with the prong running forward of the wheel and the face enclosed by the fender. They are not behind the prongs and they do not read as spheres.
