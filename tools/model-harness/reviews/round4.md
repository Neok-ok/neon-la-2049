# Round 4

Sheet: `round4.png`. Same mesh as round 3 (6,296 tris, 5.00 × 2.30 × 1.45 m). The change is the bake: the block noise is gone, exposed panels stay near the old blue-black, cavities only darken part way, and roughness is the wetness mix again. A rougher-cavity experiment on the previous bake turned the studio side into a matte black cutout and hid the chine, so that grade was rejected.

## Scores

| | Score | What the sheet actually shows |
|---|---|---|
| Silhouette | 7 | Studio 90° is a wedge, chine lit, canopy in the middle third, pod as a bulge, antenna on the tail. Studio thruster is a haunch, not a sausage. Night 90° reads the same profile. Not a box. |
| Surface detail | 6 | Nose close-up: a slit and seated breaks, not the floating plates from round 2. Thruster close-up: ring, vanes, orange core. The cable at the pod joint is still a small fin, easy to miss. Bolts do not read. |
| Lighting | 6 | The studio highlight is back on the chine and the nose. Night 90° keeps the amber strip and the roof strobe. Night rear from round 3 still applies: nozzles, tail bar, roof lights. |
| Material | 5 | Blue-black metal with a real highlight, darker in the breaks. Not the checkerboard, and not obvious grime. Pushing the cavities further killed the highlight, so this is as far as the albedo can go without flattening the form. |
| Film feel | 6 | Side, nose and thruster finally agree with each other. It is past the legacy van and past the round-1 pebble. It is still cleaner than a machine that has been flown in the rain. |
