# Round 1

Sheet: `round1.png`. Model: loft, faceted canopy, plain pods, skids, light strips. 820 tris. Measured 5.00 × 2.30 × 1.45 m. Compared with `legacy.png` (the van shell, 128 tris, 5.07 × 2.66 × 1.40 m).

## Scores

| | Score | What the sheet actually shows |
|---|---|---|
| Silhouette | 5 | Night 90° is a wedge: long nose, canopy in the middle third, tail down, a skid. Not a box and not the legacy van. Night 0° is wide and low. The pods are still sausages, and the thruster crop is a bare cylinder with no opening. |
| Surface detail | 2 | One continuous highlight. No panel break, no intake, no seam, no nozzle ring. The canopy is a pyramid dropped on the deck. |
| Lighting | 5 | White slit on the nose, amber specks on the cheeks, red and blue on the canopy, a pale flank lamp in the side view. Night holds the silhouette. Studio 90° shows the profile. The lamps are the right colours and too small at orbit distance. |
| Material | 3 | Studio is smooth black plastic. One broad highlight, no edge wear, no grime. |
| Film feel | 4 | The side view is the first frame that reads as a spinner instead of the legacy van. The surface still reads as a maquette. |

## Fix list for round 2

- Bury the pods. Rear haunch bigger than the front duct. Cut the tail off so the rear is a face, not a pebble.
- Bevel the chine and the shoulder. Do not subdivide yet.
- Open the nozzles: ring, vanes, dim core, visible in the thruster crop and from the rear orbit.
- Shoulder intakes, dark inside. Panel insets on the hood, flanks, deck and sills. A spine seam and a canopy frame so the glass does not stab the hood.
- Longer amber strips. Skid rails in the dark recess material so they separate from the belly.
