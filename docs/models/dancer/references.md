# Veil Dancer references

URLs only. Do not download the frames or the photos into git. Do not model from a performer's face, body, or costume. The stills are here for light, rain, scan, and the size of a pink figure against a person at its feet. The body in [look-target.md](look-target.md) is the body.

Our placed height stays 68 m. Write-ups below describe a screen on the order of 30 ft. That is how the shot was lit. It is not a measurement to copy onto Broadway.

## What not to bring back

A portrait. Skin. Hair. A costume copied from a still. A logo. A scanned frame used as a texture. A rescaled 9 m figure. The overlap plate is [silhouette.svg](silhouette.svg), and the critic matches the mesh to that plate.
