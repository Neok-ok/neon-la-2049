3. [lod.md](lod.md) — what Ultra, High, Medium, and Low each keep.
4. [references.md](references.md) — film stills and street photos by URL. Do not download them into git.
5. [harness-plan.md](harness-plan.md) — rounds, critic rubric, silhouette-overlap views.
