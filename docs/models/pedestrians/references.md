# Pedestrian references

URLs only. Do not download them into git. Coats, umbrellas, and the density of a wet street are the subject. Faces in the photos are not a modeling source. Signs in the photos are real businesses and stay out of the atlas.

## What not to bring back

A face. A costume copied from a still. A logo on a bag or a coat. A scanned photo used as a texture. A disc canopy. The overlap plates are [silhouette.svg](silhouette.svg).
