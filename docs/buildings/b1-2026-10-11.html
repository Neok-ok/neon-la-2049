B1 towers — 11 October 2026

Eight original tower families replace the generic financial, downtown, and Long Beach masses. Three hero megastructures stand in the west of the financial district. LAPD HQ, City Hall, and the Wallace buildings are unchanged.

What changed

The old financial body was a two-box megablock plus a compact kit tower. Downtown and Long Beach used the shared megablock kit. Those masses are gone. Streets, kiosks, crowds, and the Long Beach concourse door stay.

Field towers are 3.6 m a floor (3.4 m on the residential stack), about 150–300 m tall, so they stay under the 320 m fabric ceiling and under the 430 m sky lanes. The three heroes are landmarks, so lanes step over them: West Canyon Gate (560 m), Caldera Step (640 m), Kite Obelisk (480 m). They sit west of the opening camera, off the ray from Little Tokyo toward the Wallace pyramid. Nothing was added in the basin, and nothing was added on the south-east approach to the pyramid.

Doors, stairs, and storeys use the shared architecture helpers. The walk-through is a 2.2 m square, the same leaf LAPD and City Hall use. Rails are 1.05 m. A ground flight beside the door rises one 3.6 m storey in equal risers between 0.15 m and 0.18 m, and runs along the facade. Housing floors are 3.4 m. A 1.80 m person still fits the door. Full detail hangs one open leaf on each hero, on the civic trim sheet. The Long Beach interior door is the old concourse at the south face, not a new cut in the slab.

Graphics tiers

The building does not change size between tiers. Low keeps the masses. Medium adds balconies, fins, and decks. High and ultra add pipes, AC units, and antennas. A 1 km financial patch, counted in the harness:

Tower boxes are instances of one shared kit, so they add one draw per chunk that contains them, not a draw per tower. The three heroes are one instanced mesh per detail level. Memory

The kit box is resources.sharedGeometry('b1-kit-box'). Each chunk and each hero level clones it through resources.geometry with owner towers, so the instance buffers are counted and released on unload. The facade material is resources.material('b1-kit'). The shared template is never disposed. Every tower box, including each tread, is an instance of that 20-vertex box (96 bytes: matrix, facade data, and scale). The chunk streamer releases the clone when the chunk unloads. The three heroes release the previous level when the quality tier changes, and distant hero levels drop their GPU buffers the same way other landmarks do. Distant fabric uses the existing far mesh: detail drops away, and boxes whose tops sit under 22 m disappear. On Low the heroes keep only that mass, and the flight is dropped with the other ornament. Full detail adds one open leaf on each hero, tagged b1-door-leaf. That leaf reuses the civic trim sheet, a single 128² image, not a texture per tower. Signs and windows stay on the atlases the city already compresses to KTX2. The harness roughness map is a local canvas and is not in the game.

Lights

The lighting registry was still not on main at the last fetch. Every glow on these towers is declared in src/world/buildings/lights.ts (LIGHT_ADAPTER = 'b1-local'): door soffit, door jambs, crown, obstruction tip, ad face. The jambs sit just outside the 2.2 m opening so the doorway reads at night. A later lighting pass can map those roles without searching the recipes.

How the shapes were made

The method is the usual one for a dense game city, written here from scratch: cut a footprint into a podium and a shaft, then into floors, then hang balconies and pipes as a detail that the far view can drop. Sources, not code, were Parish and Müller on city grids (SIGGRAPH 2001), Wonka and others on facade splits (SIGGRAPH 2003), Müller and others on mass-then-facade buildings (SIGGRAPH 2006), and van Dongen’s interior mapping, which this city already runs. The tower boxes share one kit mesh through instancing. The far LOD is the impostor. Rain on the harness sheets is a painted roughness texture that stays in the harness. In the city, dirt is the stain already on the shared wall shader, and the sign and window atlases stay the compressed ones.
