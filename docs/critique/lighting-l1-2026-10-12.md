## What works

Keep these. A later pass that lifts the night sky, paints the lane magenta, or adds bloom on medium will move the picture away from the film.

- **One camera.** Night is f/2, 1/48 s, ISO 1000 (about EV 4.26, exposure about 0.044). Day is f/5.6, 1/125 s, ISO 100. Districts change which practicals are in the slots. They do not get their own exposure.
- **Darkness.** The sheet's "before" column is a pale market floor, a purple DTLA canyon, and a magenta Hollywood street. The "after" column is black walls, rain, and a few sources. K's slab with one lamp and a crowd of umbrellas is the residential frame to protect.
- **Sodium as a cutoff lantern.** 2000 K, 7 500 cd, 48° cone, about 8 m of pool from a 7 m head, so a 32 m spacing goes dark between poles. Low-pressure sodium at 589 nm is available and is not the street default. The head shell is 16 nits of amber, under the bloom threshold, so the head stays orange.
- **The registry.** `retainChunk` and `setEphemeral` run through `checkPunctual`. Bands reject bad candela, nits, kelvin, and range. `npm run lighting:check` is part of the build. Half of each depth slice stays with contact lights, so a close sign cannot take every slot.
- **Day signs actually dim.** `signPower` is 0.14 at noon and windows follow `windowLit` down to 0.12. Clear-day sky is steel `(0.22, 0.31, 0.42)`. Orange remains on dusk and on the smog weather (`smogTint` 1). Night sky and fog are held to 0.42 of the exposure lift, so the sky slot stays a night sky. Rain in the stills is still a visible stroke.
- **Where neon is allowed to tint the ground.** `neonWet` is high only on Little Tokyo (0.42), the historic core (0.36), and Hollywood (0.40). Harbor, the coast, the basin, Wallace, and the industrial belts sit at 0.02. The magenta carpet is gone.

## What fails

### Neon does not travel

A full glyph is about 1.65 times the 20-nit face, near 34 nits. Bloom starts at 32 nits and the AgX strength is 0.04, so the halo added on top of that face is on the order of a nit times 0.04. In the historic-core, Hollywood, and neon-close-up stills the tubes are sharp rectangles. Rain in front of them stays grey.

`neon-sign` and `hologram` are `volumetric: false` because a haze slot at sign candela painted a sourceless shaft down the lane. The cure removed the air next to the tube as well. A sign that does win a shade slot still fails to color the asphalt: a large blade at the 2 200 cd cap, 12 m away, leaves about a tenth of a nit on 0.045 asphalt. The 2-nit ground card (`NITS.neonReflection`) times the night exposure is a trace.

Hollywood's lane keeps a faint authored tint (`sheen` 0, `neonWet` 0.40). That tint is noise, not the tube above it. DTLA's after frame still has a purple disc under the overhead panel. The canyon around it is dark, which is the improvement. The disc is a card.

Medium and low do not bloom. The phone picture has to come from the faces and one pool. Leave the bloom chain off medium. The memory budget already names that target.

### The wet lane is black in the right way, and the wrong hue when the mirror is on

On the L1 sheet the mirror is off. Broadway, the wet-street shot, and the harbor quay show dark pavement, an orange pool, and sign color stuck on the signs. K's pool sits under the lamp and dies before the next dark stretch. That spacing is the film.

#64 then did two things the sheet does not show:

- The full-sheet grazing fresnel, which lifted a wet lane toward a pale floor, is broken into streaks. `lanternPool` paints an explicit disc: `exp(-0.11 * horizontal²)` times 14 nits (`NITS.pool * 2`). At 3 m that is about 5 nits. At 6 m it is under a third of a nit. The ellipse on current main is shorter than the broad orange foreground in `l1-wet-street.jpg`.
- The forced mirror dropped from a 0.32-scale plane at 38% opacity to a 0.18-scale plane shown only inside view-aligned ribbons (`WetReflector.ts`). Gaps stay the road. The night multiply is still toward `(0.55, 0.64, 0.78)`, so a pink sign in that ribbon is pulled toward steel. The earlier roads critique called the old forced mirror a sharp picture of the signs. The ribbon mask answers that. The steel tint spends the answer.

Puddle glow on the road mesh is still an authored magenta/cyan or sodium mix, times `neonWet`, and only inside puddle cells. On Broadway that product is about a nit at full fresnel. It cannot carry a blade sign.

### The pool is one orange, at any height

`lanternPool` uses `vec3(1, 0.45, 0.12)` for every contact practical. A 4000 K `street-led` and a future harbor mast paint the same disc. The falloff uses horizontal distance only. A practical 25 m up stamps the same 14-nit disc as a head at 7 m, as long as it occupies a contact slot.

### Harbor is the right darkness, and the hook is a trap

`harborView('street')` on the sheet: rain, a sodium pool, a dark hull, a cool block in the fog. `neonWet` is 0.02 and `streetFog` is 0.46. That is a port, not a second Broadway. Leave the weight there.

`harborMast()` returns a 2000 K practical, 4 200 cd, 58° cone, range 34 m, contact and volumetric, 60-nit shell. Nothing calls it. The live lights are one additive billboard (`src/districts/harbor/lights.ts`): white-hot core `(1, 0.96, 0.86)`, amber body, gain 1.35, and 28% of that sprite survives at noon. Three sprites per berth:

| Point | Height above ground | Reaches the quay at range 34 m? |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Mast | 25 m (`MAST_Y`) | The range includes the ground. The cone is wide. Photometric lux at the quay is a few lux. `lanternPool` would still paint a full disc, because it ignores height. |
| Boom tip | 72 m | No. The sprite is the whole light. |
| House | 81.2 m | No. Same. |

The ocean stays matte (`roughness` 0.78, `envMapIntensity` 0). The wet reflector is a 64 m quad under the camera, not clipped to the quay. Turning it on beside a slip can lay streaks onto the basin. The switch lives in `App.ts`, which the open drafts already edit.

Spinner headlights are the same kind of waiting preset: 28 000 cd, 16° cone, 90 m, volumetric. The call site is `src/vehicles/`, and K1 has those traffic files open. A beam on the wet lane is the missing practical in the chase frame. It waits.

## Refinement passes

Small, and off the files K1 (smoke, `App.ts`, vehicle traffic), B1 (towers, `cityMaterial.ts`), G1 (LAPD, City Hall, Wallace), H1 (people), and S3 (`Atmosphere.ts`, `Precipitation.ts`, `Weather.ts`, `tests/visual`) already have in draft.

### 1. Short neon air, and a halo that clears the face

Files: `src/lighting/runtime.ts`, `src/lighting/presets.ts`, `src/lighting/shade.ts`.

- Keep the bloom threshold at 32 nits so an 18-nit window and a 16-nit lamp head stay clean. Raise the AgX bloom strength from 0.04 to about 0.22. Leave medium and low unbloomed.
- Leave `neon-sign.volumetric` false at the current range (callers already override toward 8–36 m). Add a haze-only reach of about 7 m and a haze candela cap around 80, used only by `practicalInscatter`. Keep one of the four haze slots for a contact lantern.
- Do not raise `NITS.neonReflection`. A brighter ground card is a carpet.

### 2. Pool color, and height

File: `lanternPool` in `src/lighting/shade.ts`.

- Multiply by the slot rgb. A 2000 K lantern stays the orange in the K still. A 4000 K head does not.
- Include vertical distance in the falloff so a practical whose range does not reach the ground paints nothing, and a mast at 25 m paints a dim pool. Street heads near 7 m stay at today's few metres.

### 3. Wet ribbons keep the sign's hue

Files: `src/atmosphere/WetReflector.ts`, `src/roads/material.ts`.

- On the night mirror, darken with a scalar around 0.5 and drop the mix toward `(0.55, 0.64, 0.78)`. Keep `resolutionScale` 0.18 and the streak mask. Gaps stay asphalt.
- Where `sheen` is 0, tint the puddle term from the nearest non-contact shade slot. An empty slot stays black. Do not raise `neonWet`.

### 4. Harbor, after pass 2, as its own change

Files, when that pass starts: `src/districts/harbor/lights.ts` and the berth list in `sites.ts`. Not this critique.

- Register `harborMast()` at the mast only. Leave the boom tip and the house as sprites. Their height is past the 34 m range.
- Retint the sprite body to the 2000 K chromaticity, drop the white-hot core, and put the quad on the 60-nit shell times the night factor so noon does not keep a 28% beacon.
- Leave `neonWet` at 0.02. Leave the ocean matte.
- Clipping the 64 m reflector off the slips waits until `App.ts` is free.
