# B1 towers — film critique

12 October 2026. `main` at `4e438db`. The families landed in `69d41c7` (#51, 09:32 ET) and the night they sit in is L1 (#49). Docs only. No game code, no film frames, no logos.

**Overall: 6/10.** The night is a dark canyon, a 1.80 m person fits the door, and the 150–300 m band is the middle layer under the inversion. In the field the families collapse. The most common wide lot is a dark taper, the street wall is a flat plane, and the roof is bare.

#51 scored each shipped family 7–9 on blind, film, and scale, one tower in a harness frame. The numbers below are a fresh read of the merged city.

## What the film is asking the skyline to do

Published accounts, used as reference. No frames are in this commit.

- Dennis Gassner, to Denis Villeneuve, one word: brutality. The pattern language starts with the angular spinner and carries into the buildings. Scale is the power. Wallace's headquarters is described as a 300-storey mass, and the sunlight in the film belongs to that building. ([IndieWire, 23 Oct 2017](https://www.indiewire.com/awards/industry/blade-runner-2049-dystopia-production-design-denis-villeneuve-1201891427/); [Deadline, 15 Feb 2018](https://deadline.com/2018/02/blade-runner-2049-dennis-gassner-oscars-production-design-interview-1202280798/))
- John Nelson: the opening city is one packed carpet, Montreal on a bad February day, then a leap of scales (street, a much bigger middle, a much bigger top). Brutalist masses, then heavy tops: a slab sitting on a column. Signs stay restrained. The LAPD approach miniature is 1/48 and about 15 ft tall. At 4.5 m that is 216 m, the figure this repo already uses, shot as CG into a miniature into smoke. ([vfxblog, 26 Oct 2017](https://vfxblog.com/2017/10/26/lets-move-to-la-in-2049/))
- Wētā Workshop: about 38 structures, megalithic relative to a present-day street. The LAPD piece is 4.5 m and was the largest model. Pyramids were built at scales down to 1:600. Alex Funke shot them in a controlled smoke room, lights as separate passes. The buildings are lampshades: LED inside, foam and tape killing the windows that should stay dark, parallax rooms only on the faces a flyby could enter, and greebles glued on the skin. ([Wētā Workshop](https://www.wetaworkshop.com/projects/bladerunner-2049); [ACMI, workshop account](https://www.acmi.net.au/stories-and-ideas/weta-workshop-blade-runner-2049/))

This repo already encoded that leap. Fabric stays under 320 m. The hand-placed megatowers run 520–1,020 m on shafts 100–170 m wide. Wallace is 3,500 m. The inversion sits at 150–320 m, so a 200 m roof is in the soup and a 1,020 m crown comes out of it (`docs/BIBLE.md` §7.3 and the fog notes). B1's job is the carpet in that soup, with two landmarks allowed to break the ceiling. LAPD, City Hall, and Wallace stayed out of the pass, and the opening ray toward the pyramid stays clear. That placement is right. Keep it.

## What works

Keep these. A later pass that lights every floor, fattens the glow lips into window walls, or lifts the fabric through the 320 m ceiling will leave the film.

- **The before column is the picture this replaced.** Downtown was a purple slot. The financial skyline was a white fog with a few needles. The after street is a black wall, rain, one cyan lease sign (`NINTH RING`), and a person at a lit door. That is the 2049 street.
- **Human metres.** `PERSON_M` is 1.80. The opening is the civic square, 2.2 m by 2.2 m, so the headroom over that person is 0.40 m. Office floors are 3.6 m, housing floors 3.4 m. `planFlight(3.6)` is 21 risers at 0.171 m, tread 0.29 m, run 6.09 m, width 1.15 m, rail 1.05 m. The stair is beside the door, along the facade, inside the 2.2 m footprint allowance. Medium and up build it. The street cell shows it.
- **The height band.** Financial blocks are 205 × 125 m. Lots split between 55 and 125 m, then a 4–9 m gap, and the footprint is 82–92% of what remains, so the short side stays above the 26 m narrow test. Heights snap between 151.2 and 302.4 m. That is a chunky slab on the order of the 216 m LAPD miniature, and the roof stays inside the inversion. DTLA wide lots are 180–248 m. Long Beach is dimmer (`lit` 0.07–0.18) and shorter (about 61–155 m, two heroes at 180 and 165). The secondary core is quieter than downtown. Leave that dial.
- **Dark glass, warm and rare.** Night `windowLit` goes to 1. The shader then keeps 0.45 of the box's `lit`. A financial monolith is clamped to `lit` 0.12, so about 5% of its 11.7 m panels carry a pane, and that pane is about 0.8 m by 0.6 m. Window emission is `NITS.window`, 18 nits at 2700 K. Bloom starts at 32 nits, so a pane stays a point. Tower glow is that same window shader.
- **The cantilever is the heavy top Nelson described.** The stem is 40% of the lot width and the lower 28% of the height (34% on variant 1). The top is wider than the stem and still inside the footprint allowance. A 2.1 m lip marks the joint. It is also only one roll in eight.
- **The band-slab belt is thick enough to see.** A 2 m stripe vanishes past a hundred metres. These belts are 6.2 m of `Style.Solid`, every 4 storeys (14.4 m) or 6 (21.6 m). In the flyby cell the horizontal masses read.
- **Memory and tiers.** One shared box, no new texture, metres stable across tiers. Low keeps the mass. That constraint is why the street can be this empty, and it is still the right constraint.
- **What was held back.** Lantern and buttress stand in as monoliths after the kind roll, so later lots keep their seeds. The Kite Obelisk reserve is empty and the lanes still step over it. High silhouette IoU did not save them. That veto is the standard to keep.

## What fails

### The field is one taper

`pickKind` draws uniformly from eight names. Lantern and buttress are rebuilt as monoliths after the draw. On a wide lot the placed shares are:

| Family | Share | What a skyline eye gets |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Monolith | 3/8 | Five or seven steps. The last box is about a third of the base. `Style.Monolith`, one 2.4 m slit, a 5 m glow crown at 12% of the footprint. |
| Band slab | 1/8 | Inset shaft, 6.2 m belts, a 1.55 m lip on the street face and one side. Variant 2 uses `Style.Ribbon` (cell 19 m wide, window width 0.97). |
| Ziggurat | 1/8 | Five, six, or eight setbacks. The top step is about half the base. Megablock, then office glass on the last steps. A glow crown at 18% of the footprint. |
| Residential stack | 1/8 | Dark shaft. Shelves every 10.2 or 13.6 m, 1.7 m thick, each with a 1.35 m lip. |
| Holo shaft | 1/8 | A podium capped at 18 m, a shaft at 38–48% of the width, two 2.4 m corner lines, a 16 × 22 m sign. |
| Cantilever | 1/8 | The heavy top. |

Narrow lots, anything under 26 m on the short side, skip that table. They are 65% band slab and 35% residential stack, 64–120 m. Financial lots are all wide. DTLA's split lots are where the short slabs live.

A 2.4 m slit on an 80 m face is a scratch past the end of the block. The crown is 5 m of `Style.Glow` at `lit` 0.88, detail 0, so low and the far mesh both keep it. The far read of the most common tower is a dark wedge with a small lamp on its head. Ziggurats wear the same lamp. Band slabs wear lips at the same 1.55 m size as the Caldera terraces and nearly the same size as the residential shelves. At the distance the gate skyline cell uses, those are one family.

The seven megatowers already occupy these silhouettes. MT-5 is an 880 m twin on a 170 × 80 m shaft. West Canyon Gate is a 560 m twin on 70 × 48 m with a 15.4 m slot. MT-3 is a 660 m terrace on a 130 m shaft. Caldera Step is 640 m on a 70 m base, eleven equal slices, a lip on each. MT-4 is a 600 m cross with a lantern crown, the landmark version of the family #51 refused to place. MT-1 already has the raking buttresses. The new gate and the new step sit in that same west cluster, at those heights, on thinner feet. The film keeps a stepped mass and a twin mass rare. The carpet grows smaller copies.

### The street is a flat wall, and the flyby is a chart

The band-slab street cell frames the door, the person, the lease sign at 5.2 m, and one horizontal bar. The first window slice on a 14.4 m rhythm is about 8.2 m of facade under a 6.2 m belt, so the camera is on real glazing, and the glazing still reads as paint. Panel cells are 9.6 × 3.9 m. The pane is about 7.7 m wide and 0.9 m tall (`winW` 0.8, `winH` 0.22). At `lit` 0.4 about 18% of those cells burn. From the sidewalk that is a flat wall with a few short slots.

The flyby cell is the other distance. Mullions are sharp, panes are individual rectangles, rain is a streak in front of them. `farFade` averages the grid only once a cell is sub-pixel or the camera is 900–2,600 m out. A portrait at a few hundred metres never enters that fade. Living parallax on the kit material does run on medium and up, and it dies by 52 m on medium, 420 m on high, 640 m on ultra. The skyline portrait is outside the room range and inside the crisp-grid range.

Wētā put the density on the close face: parallax boxes and glued-on junk, and smoke plus a few untaped LEDs once the camera pulled back. B1's ornament is the right slot and the wrong size. Pipes, the roof box, and the three antennas are detail 2, so low and medium leave them off, and a 0.45 m antenna disappears on a 200 m shaft when high turns it on. The far mesh (`lod` 2) drops detail above 0 and drops any box whose top is under 22 m. The door, the flight, and the first shelf go away. The window chart on the mass stays.

`Style.Ribbon` on band-slab variant 2 is a glass stripe, a cell 19 m wide with the window covering 97% of it. That variant is a curtain wall. The belts are still there. The material between them is the thing Gassner's brutality was leaving behind.

### The lips tie the families together, and the rain does not soften them

Glow faces share the window nit scale. A lip at `lit` 0.7 becomes about 18 nits after `glowLevel` (`lit × 1.45` at full night). That stays under bloom, so the bar is a hard edge in the rain. The same 1.55 m lip is on every band, every Caldera terrace, and (at 1.35 m) every residential shelf. `glowFar` fills the whole quad once the face is small on screen, so a short lip becomes a continuous pinstripe at the exact distance where the window grid is supposed to dissolve.

The tall slits should stay. The monolith slit, the holo corner, and the gate's inner column are many storeys high. They are the lines that separate those masses. The short lips are the lines that make a belt, a shelf, and a terrace the same object.

DTLA also hangs an 18 × 9 m panel on 22% of wide lots, 45% along the financial edge (`holo` in `dressLot`). Nelson kept signs restrained on the city work. The holo shaft's 16 × 22 m face is the billboard that belongs to one family. The extra panels are a second pattern laid over the others.

### Roofs, from above

`dressRoof` adds one industrial box, 3–9 m wide and 2.2–5.5 m tall, at ornament 2. Residential stacks get two, plus three antennas and a riser pipe, also ornament 2. Monoliths, Caldera Step, and the unplaced obelisk skip the plant. The glow crown on the monolith is the roof, and it is on every tier.

The repo has aerials, and none of them are these families. The frames under `docs/critique/img/` are from 11 October (#34), before this merge:

- `k-megablock-aerial.jpg` is the roof B1 was told to leave alone. Flat slab, a scatter of windows, a few boxes, rain. That is already a thin roof, and it is busier than a B1 monolith.
- `financial-crown-night.jpg` is the Stage 3 kit: edge beacons, stepped crowns, cyan boards, a mast. Those landmarks are still in the city. Their pads, masts, and strobes are the roof language. The new carpet does not share it.

From a streaming horizon the carpet is flat tinted quads. The inversion hides the feet and shows those quads. A top-down at dusk would be a packing of rectangles with one lamp on the most common roof.

### The stair measures the door, and misses the housing floor

The flight always rises `STOREY` (3.6 m). A residential stack is snapped to 3.4 m, and the first shelf is at 10.2 or 13.6 m. The landing sits on blank wall, 6.6–10 m short of that shelf. The stair is a good measuring stick next to the 2.2 m hole, and it is the same measuring stick on all six placed families.

The lit plate in the opening is what makes the hole readable at night. Leave the plate at street distance. It is a porch light, and the film's doors are darker mouths. That is an acceptable trade while the wall around it is this blank. Making the plate brighter would be the wrong next move.

## Refinement passes

Three passes, all in `src/world/materials/cityMaterial.ts`, on the `b1-kit` material only (`compose` when `kit` is true). They stay off the files the open drafts already have:

- B2 and B3: `recipes.ts`, `parts.ts`, `place.ts`, `landmark.ts`, plus B3's district plans, `dtla/crowd.ts`, and the harness audit / night capture / catalog
- K1: `src/districts/_shared/kit/materials.ts` (the Stage 3 megatower kit, a different material), `App.ts`, smoke, vehicle traffic
- G1: LAPD, City Hall, Wallace
- H1: people
- S3: `Atmosphere.ts`, `Precipitation.ts`, `Weather.ts`, `tests/visual`

No new texture. No change to the cell tables in `src/world/windows/facade.ts`. Those tables feed every district.

### 1. Light whole floors, then a short run

The lit mask for kit walls is `scheduledLitNode`, which hashes each cell. Replace that mask when `kit` is true. Keep the mean at `lit * windowLit * 0.45`.

- Hash `cell.y` with the facade seed. Occupy a floor with probability `litFrac / 0.45`, clamped to 1. A financial box at `lit` 0.40 then lights about 40% of its floors.
- On an occupied floor, light one run of 2–5 bays and leave the rest of the row dark. A monolith's 11.7 m panel stays rare. Panel, Megablock, and Office gain dark storeys and a few lived-in storeys.
- Leave the non-kit graph on `scheduledLitNode`. K's slab and the ordinary fabric keep today's flicker.
- The 0.008 schedule band can stay on the edge cells of a kit run. A whole floor should not flip every minute.

### 2. Short glow quads fade with the wall

`attribute('kit')` is `(width, depth, height)`. In the kit glow path, when `kit.z` is under one glow cell (4.5 m), do not replace the face with `glowFar`. Keep `glowNear` while the quad is large on screen, and let `farFade` fall through to the concrete albedo.

That covers the 1.55 m belts, the 1.35 m shelves, the 1.55 m Caldera lips, the 2.1 m cantilever joint, and the 2.2 m door plate. The door plate remains a porch light from the sidewalk, because `farFade` is a distance term. The monolith slit, the holo corner, and the gate column are many storeys tall, so they keep a line. Band-slab's 6.2 m solid belt is `Style.Solid` and is untouched, so the horizontal mass remains after the pinstripe goes.

### 3. A pour line for the sidewalk

Monolith kit walls already draw a vertical joint, and it dies with `farFade`. Extend that joint to kit `Style.Panel`, `Style.Megablock`, and `Style.Solid`.

- Weight about 0.35, same family as the monolith joint's 0.45.
- Full strength under about 30 m, gone by about 80 m, and also multiplied by `1 - farFade`.
- The skyline portrait stays a mass and a few lights. The street cell gains a module in the concrete, which is the density low can show without a pipe mesh.

## Notes for B2 and B3

B2 is the lantern and the buttress. B3 is the Kite Obelisk and the field towers for Hollywood, the Arts District, East LA, and the South Bay. Neither of those passes should edit `cityMaterial.ts` while the three passes above are the follow-up. The notes here are what to copy and what to leave alone inside the files they already have.

**Copy**

- Stand a failed family in after the kind roll. Recipes stay. Later lots keep their seeds. The holo sign stays off the stand-in. That `r.chance` on a lantern roll is a seed consume, and it has to stay in the same place if the stand-in goes away.
- A glow box taller than one 4.5 m cell turns into a window wall. The short lip and the 2.4 m slit are the shapes that survived that bug. Keep new light lines under a cell unless the line is meant to be a shaft the height of the tower.
- Score a street frame and a flyby against the film. Envelope IoU did not predict the lantern (0.999), the buttress (0.776), or the obelisk (0.927). All three missed the picture.
- One shared kit box, no punctual light, no new texture, metres frozen across tiers, landmarks off the Wallace ray, lanes stepping over an empty reserve.

**Avoid**

- A cross that only reads from a camera on the roof. MT-4 is already a 600 m cruciform with a glazed crown. A fabric lantern that reads as a dark slab is a smaller, duller MT-4. If the cross cannot be seen from the street and from the fly, leave the monolith stand-in.
- A buttress whose core is `Style.Monolith` at `lit` 0.08. That is a blank wall from the fly. A warmer street style on the same core reads as a different building. MT-1 already owns the raking buttress. Corner piers need to stay visibly one mass with the core. The 3.2 m ties were the attempt. They did not survive the flyby.
- Replacing the stand-in with a third dark taper. Three eighths of wide lots are already the monolith. A lantern or a buttress that silhouettes as a wedge does not change the field.
- The obelisk framing trap. A close crop cut the tip. A wide crop handed the frame to a neighbour. Caldera Step is already a 640 m taper on a 70 m base in that same cluster. A 480 m kite on a 70 m base is a third needle. The tip is the silhouette. The 2.4 m seam will not carry a 480 m shaft.
- Downtown grammar in Hollywood, the Arts District, East LA, or the South Bay. East LA's own band is 10–60 m. South Bay is stacks and flares. Nelson's leap puts those districts on the low scale. A kite there fights the carpet and the Wallace ray.
- More 18 × 9 m panels. DTLA already rolls them onto 22–45% of wide lots. A new family should be a mass, and the sign should stay on the holo shaft.
- The residential stair, while `parts.ts` is open. `entryFlight` rises 3.6 m on a 3.4 m module, and the first shelf is 6.6–10 m above that landing. If the flight is touched, rise `d.storey`. Leave the 2.2 m opening, the 0.17 m riser band, and the 1.05 m rail where they are.
