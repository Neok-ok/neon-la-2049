# Critique: Sound

2026-10-10. Detail pass D2, on `cbe3064`. Docs only. No game code in this change.

The city is one sound. In the rain, nineteen of the twenty districts sit between **−21.3 and −21.6 dBFS**. Little Tokyo is the exception, at **−18.7**, because the awning hiss is loud, not because the street babble is. A dry afternoon is the same rumble plus a **13 Hz pump** that is the entire high end. Broadway’s PA, the harbor horn, Wallace’s machinery, and the sea wall are written into the district beds and then turned down until a meter cannot hear them. Rooms low-pass some highs and leave the street rumble in the room. A market downpour clips. The phone remembers mute, and it hides the control.

Levels below are dBFS at the speakers, master volume **0.8**, 44.1 kHz, the live graph (`Ambience` plus `mountCityGraph`). Bands are sub &lt; 80 Hz, low 80–250, mid 250–2 kHz, high 2–8 kHz, air &gt; 8 kHz.

## Method

Read `docs/BIBLE.md` (the D2 sound row), `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md`, and the audio path: `src/audio/mix.ts`, `graph.ts`, `CitySound.ts`, `Ambience.ts`, `beds.ts`, `synth.ts`, `capture.ts`, plus the frame wiring in `src/app/App.ts`.

`npm run build` succeeded. The production preview and the dev server were driven headless in Chromium. This VM has no discrete GPU, so WebGL is SwiftShader and `autoTier` returns **low** before the iPhone check (`src/core/quality.ts`). Phone and district listens were forced with `?quality=medium`. Ultra was not frame-timed. On medium the audio cap is 4 street voices (`VOICE_CAP` in `src/audio/registry.ts`); ultra’s extra four slots are disconnected and are the only ultra audio cost.

Offline renders, 82 of them, import the real `mix.ts` / `graph.ts` / `beds.ts` and rebuild the `Ambience` gains from `Ambience.update`. That is the evidence for levels. A live analyser on the running page matched the dry bed (−25.5 dB) and confirmed mute, the medium cap, and a resume path in Chromium. It did not match interiors: the frame loop fought a one-shot `setInterior`, so live room levels are ignored.

The layout and `registerDistrictAudio` agree on **20** districts, basin included. There is no twenty-first id to listen to. The fast camera sweep landed in 18 of them. DTLA registered when the spinner shot climbed into the polygon (camera y = 501). Wallace’s own cameras stayed in `basin-sprawl`. `dtlaView('street'|'roof'|'lanes')` returned true and left the camera in the market; the DTLA numbers below are the offline bus, plus that spinner shot. The East LA aerial preset did not leave the hall camera. Bradbury service, the Wallace atrium volume, the noodle bar, and the LAPD lobby were not entered: the first two because the camera stayed outside, the last two because they are soffits and never call `registerInterior`.

No iPhone and no Safari were in the loop. Mobile notes are the 390×844 viewport (DPR 3, coarse pointer, iPhone user agent, medium) plus what `Ambience.start` and `armResume` actually do. Frame times on SwiftShader were about 0.5–2 s, so this pass cannot claim an audio CPU cost in milliseconds. Offline renders of 1.6–5 s of audio finished in about 80–260 ms in Chrome, faster than realtime.

## What is playing

`Ambience` is the master bed: rain hiss, a patter layer, a 6 s brown city rumble, wind, a 74 Hz interior hum, machinery, surf, traffic, room noise. `CitySound` adds a drone, a murmur, an industrial texture, four PA tongues, one canyon delay, a horn, an awning hiss, one spinner voice, and up to four positional slots. Medium slot order is sizzle, murmur, neon, steam (`SLOT_KINDS`). Low (older iPhones) stops at two, so it never gets neon. Those sources are created once and left connected. Silence is a gain of 0, not a disconnected node.

The district identity lives in `src/audio/beds.ts` and is scaled by `mixTargets` in `src/audio/mix.ts`. The scale is the problem. At a rainy street, city rumble gain is **0.479** (`(0.18 + 0.3 * street)` in `Ambience.update`). Broadway PA gain is **0.071**. The harbor horn peaks at **0.044**. A financial drone is **0.042**. Those beds cannot move a mix whose floor is the rumble and the rain.

Night does not change the mix except to open the horn. Dusk DTLA in drizzle was **−23.7 dB**; dry night DTLA was **−24.3 dB**. Noon and night in the same dry district differ by about a tenth of a decibel.

## Findings

Ranked by impact per unit of work. Size is the change. iPhone risk is against the medium budget: draw calls ≤ 250, triangles ≤ 1.5 M, **4** street voices, no new audio nodes when a gain or a cutoff will do.

### 1. A dry street still pumps at 13 Hz

**Where.** Every district, any weather, including a clear basin afternoon. `src/audio/Ambience.ts` builds a 13 Hz sine into `patterGain.gain` at depth **0.25** and never scales it. `update` sets the patter target to `rain * 0.25 * street * (1 - 0.72 * interior)`, which is 0 when the sky is dry. The LFO keeps swinging the AudioParam through −0.25…+0.25.

**Evidence.** Dry afternoon high band **−33.8 dB**, peak −8.8. Same render with the LFO at 0: high **−47.5**, peak −11.2, air −58.6. The pump is the high end of a clear day. Negative gain is inaudible on noise, so what you hear is a half-wave, a 13 Hz stutter.

**Fix.** Each update, set the existing LFO depth gain to `0.45 * patterTarget`. At target 0 the depth is 0. The oscillator already exists; this is one `setTargetAtTime` on a node that is already there. Instantaneous patter gain stays between `0.55 * target` and `1.45 * target`, so it cannot go negative. Also multiply that depth by the interior amount’s complement so a closed room does not stutter.

**Acceptance.** Dry-afternoon high band ≤ **−45 dB**. Downpour peak comes down with it (finding 12). No new voice. Medium cap stays 4.

**Size S. iPhone risk: none.**

### 2. The layer you actually hear is a 6 second loop

**Where.** `Ambience` city buffer, `noise('brown', 6)`, stereo, looped, no edge fade. It is the floor under every district, inside and out.

**Evidence.** City-only render, gain 0.479: RMS **−25.8**, peak −10.8, sub −29.5. Autocorrelation **0.999 at exactly 6.0 s**, and about 0 a moment to either side. The brown integrator restarts at the seam, so the loop is a thump, not a faint chorus. The PA loop is mathematically cleaner (finding 6) and 23 dB quieter, so this seam is the one that matters now.

**Fix.** Fade about **80 ms** on both ends of the existing 6 s buffer when it is built. Keep the length. A 20 s buffer would be on the order of 7 MB of stereo float and is the wrong trade. If the excess correlation at 6.0 s stays above 0.2 after an 80 ms fade, overlap-blend that same window inside the 6 s buffer. Still built once, on the first tap.

**Acceptance.** Excess correlation at 6.0 s (correlation minus the neighbors) **under 0.2**. City-only RMS stays within 1 dB of −25.8 at gain 0.479, and within 1 dB of about **−29** after finding 5 retunes the street gain to ~0.34.

**Size S. iPhone risk: none** on the frame. The buffer is allocated once.

### 3. Rooms are a quieter street

**Where.** Master low-pass in `Ambience.update`: `max(280, 15000 - m * 14720)`. City duck is only `(1 - 0.5 * m)`. Room gain is `interior * 0.02`. Hum is `hum * 0.016` on one 74 Hz sine. The patter LFO ignores the room.

Lived-in checks: K apartment (`at` the apartment, muffle spec 0.88, hum 0.45), K corridor (0.92 / 0.7), Bradbury court (0.8), Wallace atrium (0.9 / 0.45), LAX concourse (0.75). The long preview shots settled at **0.80** in the court and **0.88** in the apartment. Fast hops under-settled because SwiftShader frames are slow and dt is clamped at 0.1 s; trust the settled shots and the offline law.

**Evidence.** K apartment: muffle **2046 Hz**, RMS −28.4 against a −21.2 street, high band still −36.9, hum gain **0.007** against a city gain of 0.268. Bradbury court: **3224 Hz**, high −32.8, only 6 dB under Broadway. LAX concourse: **3960 Hz**. The service corridor (muffle 1, 280 Hz, high −53) shows the filter can close; almost no authored room asks for muffle 1, and the linear curve leaves 0.8–0.9 up in the speech range. The LFO still runs at ±0.25 indoors.

**Fix.** One cutoff, in the same biquad:

`frequency = max(280, 14000 * (260 / 14000) ** muffle)`

That is **419 Hz at muffle 0.88** and **577 Hz at 0.8**, which is the trial. Duck the city bed with `(1 - 0.84 * m)` instead of `(1 - 0.5 * m)`. Room gain `interior * (0.04 + 0.05 * rain)`. Hum gain `hum * 0.05` (the apartment’s 0.45 becomes **0.023**, level with a ducked city bed around 0.03–0.09). Scale the patter LFO by the patter target so it dies indoors (finding 1).

Do not also drop the *street* city gain to 0.10. With street city at 0.34 (finding 5) and this duck, the apartment city gain is about **0.09**. The trial’s 0.031 was quieter than this and measured −35.2 / high −60.5. If the remeasure lands louder than −32, take the duck to 0.90. If it lands quieter than −36, ease the duck. The room and the hum have to be the thing you hear, with the street as a low remainder.

**Acceptance.** K apartment high band ≤ **−55 dB**, RMS about **−32 to −36**, hum gain about 0.02. Bradbury court, muffle 0.8, stays louder than the apartment (it is a court with a slit of street) and at least 8 dB under Broadway. Service, muffle 1, stays the dead room, at the 280 Hz floor.

**Size M. iPhone risk: none.** Same biquad, same oscillators, new numbers.

### 4. Rain does not care how high you are

**Where.** `Ambience.update` multiplies rain by weather and interior only. Patter fades with the street factor; the hiss does not. The rain *mesh* already uses `aboveClouds = clamp(1 - (cam.y - 1400) / 600, 0, 1)` in `App.ts`. Audio never reads it.

**Evidence.** Spinner at 220 m, rain 0.65: hiss gain **0.208**, the street value, high band −27.5, RMS −23.2. Aerial at 1600 m with `aboveClouds` 0.15: hiss still **0.208**, high −27.6. Crest of the wall, alt ~90: hiss 0.208, RMS −21.8. A rooftop at 85 m is −22.2 with full rain and no PA.

**Fix.** Pass the same `aboveClouds` into the bed. Multiply rain and patter by:

`altClose = alt < 30 ? 1 : alt < 520 ? (520 - alt) / 490 : 0`

and by `aboveClouds`. Suggested hiss coefficient **0.22** (today 0.32), patter coefficient **0.18** (today 0.25), still times the interior duck. No floor that keeps 35% of the hiss at altitude: at 500 m the hiss should be gone, and the cloud factor takes what remains above the deck. Crest and rooftop stay in the weather (`altClose` ≈ 0.9 at 90 m).

**Acceptance.** Spinner-fly and the 1600 m aerial: high band at least **6 dB** under a street in the same rain. The trial aerial, which did this multiply, landed at high **−43.8**. Street rain RMS should stay near **−20 to −23**, not the trial’s collapsed bed.

**Size S. iPhone risk: none.**

### 5. Twenty districts, one mix

**Where.** `mixTargets` in `src/audio/mix.ts`, and the `Ambience` city/rain coefficients. The beds in `beds.ts` already disagree with each other: Broadway canyon 0.95 and PA 1, basin PA 0.05, harbor foghorn 1, Wallace industrial 0.8, LAX PA 0.36 with crowd share 0. The scaler throws that away.

Street city gain 0.479 against drone `bed.drone * 0.05` (financial **0.042**, basin **0.012**), PA `bed.pa * 0.085 * …`, industrial `bed.industrial * 0.032`. A district-only Broadway render is **−33 dB**. The full rainy street is −21.

**Evidence.** The table above. Rainy RMS span outside the market is **0.3 dB**. Dry span is **0.6 dB**. East LA’s hall is −18.9 because `inMarket` opens the awning hiss (0.176), the same trick as Little Tokyo, while its PA sits at 0.026.

**Fix.** Retune in `mix.ts` and `Ambience.update` only. One law, shared with the offline capture.

- Street city `(0.12 + 0.22 * street)` → **0.34** on the ground, about −3 dB from today. City-only today is −25.8 at 0.479; at 0.34 it lands near **−29**. That is the weight to keep. The trial’s `(0.05 + 0.07 * street) * 0.85` (noon) is **0.10** and measured **−37.8**. Do not ship it.
- Rain hiss **0.22**, patter **0.18**, times finding 4’s altitude and cloud factors.
- Drone coefficient **0.05 → 0.14**, and multiply by `(0.65 + 0.70 * night)` so noon and night on the drone differ by about 6 dB. Financial at night ≈ 0.16, within about 6 dB of city 0.34. Basin ≈ 0.05, clearly quieter. The drone node is already a brown loop into a 150 Hz low-pass. **Do not add a sub sine.** The trial added one (`36 + bed.drone * 22` Hz) and it did not replace the lost rumble.
- Leave murmur and industrial coefficients alone until the remeasure. If Wallace / LAX / the refineries still fail finding 9, raise industrial one step, still a gain.

**Acceptance.** Dry city-only near **−29 dB**, not −38. Rainy Little Tokyo remains the loud street, and a rainy basin stays at least **3 dB** under it once the market awning is the difference rather than the whole mix. Full-bus peak on a rainy street ≤ **−3 dBFS**. Medium cap stays 4. No samples, no new nodes.

**Size M. iPhone risk: low**, provided the pass does not connect the high/ultra slots on medium and does not allocate new oscillators.

### 6. The PA is filtered into a hole, then looped every 1.6 s

**Where.** `tongueBuffer` in `src/audio/synth.ts` builds four 1.6 s formant buffers (20 ms edge fade). Tongue 1, Broadway and Hollywood, is formants **280 / 1760 Hz**. Playback in `mountCityGraph` then band-passes at `780 + i * 140` (**920 Hz** for tongue 1), which sits between the formants. `CitySound` sends that into a delay of `0.11 + canyon * 0.1` (Broadway **0.205 s**) with feedback `0.16 + canyon * 0.22` (Broadway **0.369**).

LAX authors PA 0.36, and the presence term uses a floor only when `bed.pa > 0.4`. Crowd share for LAX is 0, so the gain collapses to **0.012**. K is authored at exactly 0.4 and misses the same floor.

**Evidence.** PA-only, Broadway tongue, 8 s: RMS **−49.1**, peak −33.9, autocorrelation **1.000 at 1.6 s**. The loop is perfect and currently harmless, because it is 28 dB under the rainy street (−20.9). Raising the fader without moving the filter will chant. The trial raised the PA coefficient only to 0.1 (Broadway gain 0.086, +1.7 dB) and did not retune the filter; Broadway got quieter because the rain and the city were cut, not because the PA arrived.

**Fix.** Remove the playback band-pass, or set each tongue’s playback frequency to its **f2** (1280, 1760, 980, 1540). The buffer is already formant-filtered. Lengthen the four buffers to **4 s** (about 2.8 MB of mono float, once). 8 s is about 5.6 MB; use it only if 4 s still reads as a chant. Keep the edge fade. Presence floor: `Math.max(presence, bed.pa * 0.45)` so an authored PA speaks with an empty crowd. Canyon delay `0.16 + canyon * 0.22` (Broadway ~0.37 s), feedback `0.10 + canyon * 0.16` capped near **0.25** (the trial’s Broadway feedback was 0.252 and did not clip). Then set the PA coefficient so the **solo** Broadway tongue lands near **−32 dB** RMS. Do not multiply 0.085 by a constant until that solo exists. If removing the filter alone gets there, leave 0.085.

**Acceptance.** Broadway PA-only RMS about **−32 dB** (±2), peak ≤ −10, excess correlation at the buffer length under 0.2. LAX street PA gain at least 4× today’s 0.012. Feedback ≤ 0.28 anywhere. Still four tongue sources, no fifth.

**Size M. iPhone risk: low.** The cost is a one-time buffer, not a voice. Stay inside the 4-slot pool.

### 7. The foghorn does not change the night

**Where.** `mixTargets`: `hornEnv * bed.foghorn * night * hornStreet * 0.045`. `hornStreet` is gone by **180 m**. Harbor bed foghorn is 1, coast 0.28, south bay 0.18, long beach 0.12. The oscillator is a 52 Hz sine plus noise, already built. Envelope 0.35 s attack, hold to 2.1, release to 4.6, period 41 s (`hornEnvelope`).

**Evidence.** Harbor night RMS −22.5, day −22.8. Sub **−28.5 vs −29.1**. Horn peak **0.044** against city 0.476. At the crest the peak is 0.006.

**Fix.** Coefficient **0.18** as the first remeasure, not 0.045 and not the trial’s 0.11. The trial’s 0.108 put the sub in front only while city gain was 0.12. Against a street city of 0.34, 0.11 sits about 10 dB down and will disappear again. Fade the horn out by **400 m**, not 180. Coast, south bay, and long beach already scale by `bed.foghorn`; leave those fractions so they stay quieter than the harbor.

**Acceptance.** During the hold, harbor-night sub band ≥ **3 dB** above harbor-day in the same rain, peak ≤ **−3 dBFS**. If 0.18 overshoots the peak, back off. If the sub still does not move, one more step, still the same oscillator.

**Size S. iPhone risk: none.**

### 8. The sea wall sounds like any wet street

**Where.** Surf gain `surf * 0.075` in `Ambience.update`. Coastal bed is almost empty (drone 0.22, PA 0, foghorn 0.28).

**Evidence.** Sea wall RMS **−21.4**, the generic rainy-street number. Surf gain 0.068 against city 0.477. Crest wind is already 0.397 and the RMS only moves to −21.8. There is no solo surf render, so the coefficient below is a starting point with a test, not a tuned fader.

**Fix.** First step `surf * 0.22` (about +9 dB), same node, still ducked indoors by the existing `(1 - 0.45 * interior)`. Remeasure the wall against `rain-basin-sprawl` at the same rain. Acceptance: wall low band ≥ **3 dB** above the inland street (today the inland low band is about −30). Peak ≤ −3 dBFS. If 0.22 misses, one more gain step. Do not add a breaker sample.

**Size S. iPhone risk: none.**

### 9. Launch rumble and the Wallace machines are inaudible

**Where.** `setMachinery`. Noise `mach * 0.04`, tone `mach * 0.01` at 41 Hz-ish (`machTone`). Wallace street passes 0.82, LAX launch was rendered at 0.85, harbor hum and the south-bay hum add in through `App.ts`.

**Evidence.** LAX launch sub **−28.7** against a dry street near −29. Tone gain 0.009, mach 0.03, city 0.479. Wallace street tone 0.008, RMS −23.0, the rainy-street ballpark. The trial’s tone coefficient 0.028 still left Wallace at −29.7 because the city bed had been pulled out; that does not prove +9 dB of tone is enough against city 0.34.

**Fix.** First step, noise coefficient **0.04 → 0.22**, tone **0.01 → 0.06**. Same two nodes. Acceptance: Wallace-street and LAX-launch sub band ≥ **3 dB** above a dry street in that district, peak ≤ −3 dBFS. One more step if the sub does not move. No new oscillator.

**Size S. iPhone risk: none.**

### 10. Neon is attenuated twice, and it dies a few meters out

**Where.** `slotLevel` for neon: base `0.12 * bed.neon`, reach **7 m**, then squared distance. The panner is `inverse` with ref 1.1, rolloff **2.4**, max 8 (`SLOT_FILTER` in `graph.ts`). Medium connects this slot (third of four). Low never does.

**Evidence.** Broadway, neon 2.6 m from the listener: pre-panner gain **0.047**. Panner gain at that distance is about `1.1 / (1.1 + 2.4 * (2.6 - 1.1))` ≈ **0.23**, so the buzz arrives near **0.011**. Market neon pre-panner is 0.043. The sign is in the shot and not in the mix.

**Fix.** `rolloffFactor * 0.35` on the existing panners (neon 2.4 → 0.84). Neon base **0.12 → 0.22**, reach **7 → 12**. Replace the squared cutoff with `1 / (1 + (dist / reach) ** 2)` so a source at the edge is quiet rather than hard-cut; the trial did this and the market peak stayed at −4.2 under a hotter base (0.26). Start at 0.22. If a market remeasure peaks hotter than −3 dBFS, drop the base before touching rain. Do not spend a fifth medium slot, and do not pull neon into the low tier’s two slots.

**Size S. iPhone risk: none** on medium. Low-tier phones still have no buzz; that is the cap, and it should stay.

### 11. Wallace’s causeway plays the basin

**Where.** `CitySound.follow` sets the bed from `layout.districtAt` only. `App.ts` treats Wallace as the polygon **or** the pyramid reserve plus 40 m, and that test drives machinery and fog. The bible notes the district edge cuts the plinth, so the causeway and the north court sit in the reserve.

### 13. The spinner you are flying has no engine

### 14. A second district boundary throws away the crossfade

**Where.** `CitySound.follow`. Tau is 1.35 s, which is fine for walking. On a new id the code sets `bW = aW` and `aW = 0`, discarding whatever was still in `bW`. `blendBeds` divides by `aw + bw`, so drone and PA *targets* do not dip. Tongue gains are `mix.pa * weight` with the raw weights, so they do.

**Evidence.** Simulated, same math as `follow`: DTLA settled, 0.45 s into historic-core, 0.45 s into the market. Sum of weights falls from 1 to **0.487** (`aW` 0.283, `bW` 0.203). Inaudible today because the PA is −49 dB. It becomes a hole in the canyon as soon as finding 6 raises the tongue.

**Fix.** On a retarget, `bW = aW + bW`, then `aW = 0`. Weight is conserved. If the sum ever exceeds 1, scale both. Do not shorten the 1.35 s tau.

**Size S. iPhone risk: none.**

### 15. iOS can lose the context, and the resume path does not cover it

**Where.** `Ambience.start`. If `this.ctx` already exists it calls `resume()`. On the first call it constructs `AudioContext` inside the gesture and does not. `armResume` resumes only when `state === 'suspended'`. It does not resume `'interrupted'`. The `visibilitychange` listener calls `resume()` with no user gesture.

The title card’s Fly / Walk / Watch buttons call `start()` inside the tap, which is the right gesture when there is no `mode` in the URL. A `?mode=` link skips the card (`showTitle = !urlMode`) and the first `pointerdown` / `keydown` unlocks instead.

**Evidence.** Chromium, including the 390×844 page: a context constructed here came up `running` (autoplay allowed), `suspend()` took it to `suspended`, and a `visibilitychange` brought it back to `running`. That is not Safari. iOS drops a running context to `interrupted` on lock, and `resume()` from `visibilitychange` is rejected there. Mute itself persisted: after the Sound control was toggled, `localStorage['nla.audio']` was `{"muted":true,"volume":0.8}` across reload, and the master landed at 0 (immediately after the click it was still 0.006, because `setTargetAtTime` uses a 0.04 s time constant).

**Fix.** After `new AudioContext()`, call `void ctx.resume()` in that same turn. In `armResume`, resume when the state is `suspended` **or** `interrupted`. Keep the pointer / touch / key listeners. Treat `visibilitychange` as a hint that runs only if the state is `suspended` and the browser actually accepts it; do not describe it as the iOS unlock. This still needs a device pass. The code change is what the phone will require.

**Size S. iPhone risk: none.** A failed `resume()` is the risk of leaving it as it is.

**Size S. iPhone risk: none.**

### 17. The phone page threw `setTargetAtTime` twice

### 18. The four demo WAVs are not the game

### 20. Every frame retargets gains that have not moved

**Where.** `aim` calls `setTargetAtTime` unconditionally. `Ambience.update` does the same for rain, patter, city, wind, hum, mach, surf, traffic, room, and the cutoff, every frame, including when the value is unchanged.

**Evidence.** Not a measured jank. SwiftShader frames were too slow to see an audio cost. The graph is on the order of the ambience bed plus the city graph: the looping sources are created once and kept, and medium runs four slots. The cheap win is to stop scheduling a new automation event when the target has not moved by ~0.001 (or ~1 Hz for a filter). **Fix.** In `aim` and the `Ambience` writers, skip when the new target matches the last scheduled value within 0.001. Finding 17’s finite guard belongs in the same function.

**Size S. iPhone risk: none**, and it is the one change aimed at the audio thread.

## Refinement passes

Four passes. Each is one agent’s job, inside the medium budget: no new sample, no new oscillator, voice cap stays 4, draw calls and triangles untouched. Re-render the scenes named in the findings after each pass. The trial mix in the levels section is the boundary: if a dry street falls near **−38 dB**, the city bed was cut too far.

### Pass A — Silence the dry pump and the 6 s seam

In `Ambience.ts`, scale the existing 13 Hz patter LFO depth to `0.45 * patterTarget` (0 when the sky is dry, and 0 when the interior duck has killed the patter), so the gain cannot swing negative. Fade about 80 ms at both ends of the existing 6 s brown city buffer; if the excess correlation at 6.0 s stays above 0.2, overlap-blend that window without lengthening the buffer. In `start()`, call `resume()` in the same turn as `new AudioContext()`, and in `armResume` resume both `suspended` and `interrupted`. Acceptance: dry-afternoon high band ≤ −45 dB (it was −33.8, and −47.5 with the LFO forced off), city-only excess correlation at 6 s under 0.2, no new voices, medium cap still 4. Do not retune district gains in this pass.

### Pass B — Rooms and height

Replace the linear interior cutoff with `max(280, 14000 * (260 / 14000) ** muffle)` (419 Hz at K’s 0.88, 577 Hz at the Bradbury court’s 0.8). Duck the city bed with `(1 - 0.84 * m)` instead of `(1 - 0.5 * m)`. Set room gain to `interior * (0.04 + 0.05 * rain)` and hum gain to `hum * 0.05`. Multiply rain and patter by an altitude close (full below 30 m, gone by about 520 m) and by the same `aboveClouds` factor the rain mesh already uses, and drop the hiss coefficient from 0.32 to 0.22 and patter from 0.25 to 0.18. In fly mode, when no flyby is louder than about 0.04, hold the existing spinner voice at gain 0.04 and about 160 Hz. If the noodle bar or the LAPD lobby already has a camera-inside test, push the existing muffle blend to about 0.35 there; do not build a new interior. Acceptance: K apartment high band ≤ −55 dB and RMS about −32 to −36; the court stays louder than the apartment and at least 8 dB under Broadway; spinner and aerial high bands drop at least 6 dB from a street in the same rain; fly mode keeps a steady turbine. No new nodes. If the apartment lands louder than −32, take the city duck to 0.90; if it lands quieter than −36, ease it. Do not get there by cutting the street city bed to 0.10.

### Pass C — Make the districts audible

Keep the street city gain near **0.34** (`0.12 + 0.22 * street` in `Ambience.update`). The trial at ~0.10 measured −37.8 dB on a dry afternoon and lost the low end; that number is a wall, not a target. Raise the drone coefficient from 0.05 to 0.14 and multiply by `(0.65 + 0.70 * night)`. Retune PA in this order: point each tongue’s playback filter at that tongue’s f2 or remove it, lengthen the four tongue buffers to 4 s with the edge fade kept, set the presence floor to `max(presence, bed.pa * 0.45)`, set canyon delay to `0.16 + canyon * 0.22` and feedback to `0.10 + canyon * 0.16` (cap ~0.25), then choose the PA coefficient so a Broadway PA-only render is about −32 dB RMS. Horn coefficient 0.18, fading by 400 m rather than 180 m. Surf coefficient 0.22. Machinery noise 0.22 and tone 0.06. Neon rolloff ×0.35, base 0.22, reach 12 m. On a district change, set `bW = aW + bW` so a second boundary does not throw the crossfade away. In `follow`, use the Wallace reserve test `App.ts` already uses for machinery, so the causeway is `wallace-vernon` rather than `basin-sprawl`. All of this is gains, one filter, and one id, in `mix.ts`, `Ambience.ts`, `synth.ts`, `graph.ts`, and `CitySound.ts`. Re-render market downpour (peak ≤ −3 dBFS, 0 clips), Broadway solo, harbor night against harbor day (sub band ≥ 3 dB during the blast), the sea wall against a basin street (low band ≥ 3 dB), LAX launch and Wallace street (sub ≥ 3 dB above a dry street there).
