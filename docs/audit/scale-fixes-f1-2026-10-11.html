Building scale fixes — 11 October 2026

Doors, stairs, rails and storeys brought onto a 1.80 m person. Landmark silhouettes stay. This page is the fix record for the S1 audit at docs/audit/scale-2026-10-11.html.

How the metres were chosen

Engine units are already metres. A flight takes the floor-to-floor height, splits it into equal risers inside 0.15–0.18 m, then sets the going from 2×rise + tread ≈ 0.63 m, clamped to 0.28–0.32 m. The run grows by adding steps. A step is never stretched to fill a span, including on the mid LOD. Far LOD may omit the flight. The walking dimensions do not change with Ultra, High, Medium or Low.

A walk-through opening is 2.2 m high and about 2.1–2.2 m wide. A grand frame may be taller (Bradbury’s pale frame is 3.2 × 3.6 m around a 2.2 m hole; heritage portals clamp at 3.2 × 3.6 m). Handrails sit 1.05 m above the walking surface. Storey cells sit in 3–4 m. Slit (13 m) and Monolith (11.7 m) stay tall panels. Glow stays 4.5 m.

The helpers live in src/world/architecture/scale.ts. A painted 128×128 trim sheet and an open door leaf live in src/world/architecture/trim.ts. Neither file copies a published sample. The leaf is full detail only and has no collider, so the path stays open.

Bible deltas

The footbridge bible row said 32 × 0.35 m. This pass uses 66 real risers. Bradbury’s stone front is still 22.4 m; it now reads as seven 3.2 m storeys instead of five 4.4 m ones. The Long Beach concourse shell is 14.4 m (4 × 3.6), not the old “15 m is 3 × 5.0”. docs/BIBLE.md was left alone. tools/scale-audit/measure.ts anchors were left alone; they are the historical snapshot and will throw if re-run.

Draw calls

Metres do not change with the quality tier. Ornament does. Counts below are the live scene at the camera, so traffic and streaming move them. High, before this pass, was about 201 draws / 607k triangles on the Little Tokyo street, 197 / 630k at the Bradbury door, and 178 / 467k on the LAPD steps. After, at Medium: Little Tokyo 157 draws / 568k triangles, Bradbury door 174 / 669k, LAPD steps 208 / 649k. The footbridge, shot from a tread, is 148 draws / 464k triangles. The extra triangles against the old flight are the added risers (32 steps became 66). A High Little Tokyo frame after the change was 224 draws / 693k triangles. Ultra at the Bradbury door was 264 draws / 837k. Low at that same door was 460 draws / 539k: more draws, fewer triangles, same 2.2 m hole.
